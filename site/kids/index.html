FAQ

Questions from parents

My child has never played. Is that OK?

Yes. Most kids start from zero, and lessons begin with the basics.

Can siblings or friends share a lesson?

Yes. 2 kids is $70/hour each, and a group of 4 is $50/hour each.

Do we need to buy a racquet?

Not to start. Racquets and balls are provided. The coach can advise on the right racquet size later if your child keeps going.

When are lessons?

Weekday evenings from 7pm, and from 7am on weekends and public holidays.

Who books the court?

You do, at any court near you. The coach comes to you.
